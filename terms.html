App Store license

The license that applies to your App Store download is the agreement identified by the store for that download. Apple provides a standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement when a developer has not supplied a custom agreement. This page provides references and usage information; it does not create a separate custom license.

What Glasswitch does

Glasswitch is a macOS utility for choosing the system default browser among installed compatible browsers. macOS may require confirmation. Available browsers depend on your Mac. Light, Dark, language, and login preferences are configured in the app.
