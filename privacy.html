Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026 · 2026 年 9 月 5 日生效

English

Browser Switcher is an independent macOS utility.

Browser Switcher does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app requires no account and contains no advertising, trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs.

The app locally reads the current default browser and basic application information for installed compatible browsers. Language, launch-at-login, and other preferences are stored on your Mac.

Browser Switcher does not read or store browsing history, webpage content, cookies, passwords, or bookmarks.

This website uses no developer-added analytics or advertising cookies.

中文

Browser Switcher 是一款独立的 macOS 工具。

Browser Switcher 不收集、传输、出售或共享个人数据。应用不要求创建账户，也不包含广告、跟踪器或第三方分析 SDK。

应用会在设备本地读取当前默认浏览器，以及已安装兼容浏览器的基本应用信息。语言、登录启动及其他偏好保存在你的 Mac 上。

Browser Switcher 不读取或保存浏览历史、网页内容、Cookie、密码或书签。

本网站不使用由开发者添加的分析工具或广告 Cookie。
